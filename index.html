⚫ Task 1 — Threshold & Paths
Goal: make your image high-contrast, then turn the shapes into a path for text to follow.

Steps — click to expand

1
Set Up Your Document
▶
  • New document: File → New — A3, 300dpi
  • Import your photocopier or scanner experiment
  • Duplicate your layer: Cmd/Ctrl + J

💡 Tip

  • Always work on the duplicate. Keep your original safe.
2
Apply Threshold
▶
  • Select your duplicated layer
  • Image → Adjustments → Threshold
  • Move the slider. Click OK.
What Threshold does: makes your image pure black and white. No grey. This gives you clear shapes.
3
Make a Selection
▶
  • Magic Wand tool: W
  • Click a black or white shape you like
  • Hold Shift to add more areas
Two other ways to select:

Quick Selection — drag over an area

Color Range — selects by colour

4
Turn Your Selection Into a Path
▶
  • Right-click your selection → Make Work Path
  • Tolerance: 1.0 pixels. Click OK.
  • Open Window → Paths to see it
  • Double-click "Work Path" to save it with a name

Task 1 Checklist ✓

Created A3 document and duplicated layer
Applied Threshold adjustment
Made a selection with Magic Wand

✍️ Task 2 — Add Text on Path
Goal: add text that follows the path you made in Task 1.

Steps — click to expand

1
Select the Type Tool
▶
  • Press T
  • Select your path in the Paths panel first
2
Click on the Path
▶
  • Move your cursor near the path
  • Wait for the cursor to change
  • Click where you want the text to start
Cursor not changing? Check your path is selected in the Paths panel.
3
Type Your Text
▶

Your text will follow the path. Try:

  • An artist quote — from an artist you like
  • Keywords — from your Term 2 project

🎨 Task 3 — Blend Modes & Artist References
Goal: style your text and add a proper artist citation.

Steps — click to expand

1
Apply a Blend Mode to Your Text
▶
  • Select your text layer in the Layers panel
  • Find the blend mode dropdown — it says "Normal"
  • Try different modes. Adjust opacity if needed.
2
Add an Artist Reference
▶
  • Add the artist's name with your quote
  • Add a Harvard reference at the bottom of your design
3
Final Check
▶
  • Check your text is readable
  • Save your PSD with all layers
  • Export a JPEG for submission

Blend Mode Ideas

Multiply

Darkens. Good on white backgrounds.

Screen

Lightens. Good on dark backgrounds.

Overlay

High contrast effect.

Difference

Dramatic colour change.

4. Technique 1: Document Setup & Threshold ⭐ 10 min

Step 1-2: Prepare Your Image & Apply Threshold

1. Document Setup

  • Create a new document (File → New)
  • Set size to A3, and resolution to 300dpi
  • Import your photocopier or scanner experiment
  • Duplicate your layer (Ctrl/Cmd+J)

Pro Tip: Always work on a duplicate layer to keep your original safe!

2. Make High Contrast

  • Select your duplicated layer in the Layers panel
  • Go to Image → Adjustments → Threshold
  • Move the slider to adjust the black/white balance
  • Click OK when you're happy with the result

Threshold makes your image pure black and white with NO gray tones. This makes it easier to create clear paths.

Step-by-Step Tutorial: Document Setup & Threshold
Why This Matters: The Threshold adjustment creates clear, high-contrast shapes that are perfect for creating paths. This clean black and white image will make selection much easier in the next step.
5. Technique 2: Create Selections & Convert to Paths ⭐ 15 min

Step 3-4: Make Selections & Convert to Paths

3. Make Selections

  • Select the Magic Wand tool (W)
  • Click on black areas or white areas you want to use
  • Hold Shift to add more areas
  • Experiment with selecting different shapes

Look for areas with interesting shapes that would work well with text following their edges.

4. Convert to Paths

  • With your selection active, right-click
  • Choose Make Work Path from the menu
  • Set Tolerance to 1.0 pixels for smooth paths
  • Click OK

Your path will appear in the Paths panel. If you don't see the Paths panel, go to Window → Paths to show it.

Path Panel Tips:

Saving Your Path

Double-click the "Work Path" in the Paths panel and give it a name to save it.

Multiple Paths

You can have multiple paths for different text elements.

Selecting Paths

Click on a path in the Paths panel to make it active before adding text.

6. Technique 3: Add Text on Path & Apply Blend Modes ⭐ 20 min

Step 5: Add Text on Path

Adding Text to Your Paths

  • Select the Type tool (T)
  • Move your cursor near the path until it changes to a curved text icon
  • Click on the path to position your text
  • Type your text - it will follow the path!

Troubleshooting: If your cursor doesn't change to the curved text icon, make sure your path is selected in the Paths panel.

Step 7: Apply Blend Modes

Make Your Text Blend with Your Image:

  • Select your text layer in the Layers panel
  • Try different blend modes from the dropdown menu
  • Adjust the opacity slider if needed
Multiply
Screen
Overlay
Difference
Quick Blend Mode Guide:
  • Multiply: Good for white backgrounds - darkens where layers overlap
  • Screen: Good for dark backgrounds - lightens where layers overlap
  • Overlay: Creates high contrast while preserving colors
  • Difference: Creates dramatic color inversions - good for experimental effects
